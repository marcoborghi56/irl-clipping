## Patterns in this batch
- Short or tight outliers (8-20s) held a single beat: 4 of 8 outliers (out12, out13, out15, plus out09 at 39s with one arc) versus the flops at 11-110s with no beat (3 of 3).
- Outliers have a human-to-human beat with strangers, celebrities or a spoken sincere moment (out10, out12, out13): 3 of 8; flops have none (0 of 3).
- Hook is cold: speech or a question from 0.0s ("Can I get a fry over?", "Are you going to leave at 3am...", the quoted name-change question): 5 of 8 outliers; flop hooks are descriptive captions with a spoken filler line (3 of 3).
- Static caption states a framing/curiosity gap ("Shxrk after losing FNCS Globals...", "Generational switch up", `"Why did Ron change his name..."`): 6 of 8 outliers use a story-framing title; flops use labels (3 of 3 describe scene rather than tease).
- Payoff lands mid-to-late with a clear line ("mental health", "That's a good ass name"): 5 of 8 outliers at 30-85% of runtime.
- Many outliers are montage edits with meme insertions and fast cuts (out09, out14, out16): 3 of 8; many are not IRL, so treat this as edit style, not moment selection.
- Raw-footage flags that matter for IRL: spoken lines said directly to strangers, laughter, shouting spikes (out13 2.0-6.5s, out14 25-27s), celebrity reveal (out12).
- Flops are low in spoken content or contain nothing actionable (0.26 and 0.37 words/s in flop04 and flop06): 2 of 3.
