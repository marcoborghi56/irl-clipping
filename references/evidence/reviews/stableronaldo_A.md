## Patterns in this batch
- Outliers carry a human, emotional storyline that needs no game knowledge (raid rescue, crying friend, sneaking off at night, trapped friend screaming): 6 of 8 outliers. The three flops are game cutscenes, a name-drop montage and an in-joke about a game opponent.
- A one-line caption that names the story in plain words ("A raid changed his life...", "First night without Los...", "Where does Los go at night?", "It's soundproof"): 7 of 8 outliers. Flops use vague or insider captions ("Bepsy is back...", "Define Betrayal.", "I can't do Nathan"), 3 of 3.
- The caption stays static for the full reel at the lower third, with an emoji: 8 of 8 outliers, 3 of 3 flops. This does not separate them; it is baseline.
- Real-person IRL moments with a spike (screaming, shouting, crying) clear the bar best. out03 has audio flags for shouting and laughter at 4 spans, and out01 has a mum shouting. Flops have no shouting spike aside from one at 33s in flop01: 2 of 8 outliers clearly, 0 of 3 flops.
- Runtime does not separate them. Outliers run 11s to 112s, flops 10s to 41s. Payoff timing does: outliers' turn lands by 30-60% of runtime, while flop01 lands at about 95%: 6 of 8 outliers.
- Cut rate is split. out03 cuts 15 per 10s and out08 cuts 1.6. A clipper should match cut rate to the footage (whip pans for a physical prank, long holds for talk): mixed.
- Music is present in 7 of 8 outliers (out03 is the exception, where natural screams carry the clip). Flops also have it, so music alone does not help.
- Recurring cast and a named person in the caption (Los, Camden, Adapt) feature in 4 of 8 outliers, but the flops use names too (Bepsy). The difference is that outlier captions state what happened to them.
