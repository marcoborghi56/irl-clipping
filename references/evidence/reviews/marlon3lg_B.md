- Outlier moments are social collisions or crossovers (strangers, Zlatan, KSI, a crowd, an NFL door, a dementia stranger): 7 of 8 outliers (out10, out13-out16 and out11, out12 partly); flops are solo monologue, product handoff or a day summary: 3 of 3 flops.
- The caption sets up the premise in one line, with emoji, often with a name ("Marlon helps...", "Marlon and Zlatan..."): 6 of 8 outliers; flops use captions too (3 of 3), so the difference is in the content, not the caption.
- The payoff in outliers sits late and hits one line or reveal (face-off, reunion, Zlatan punchline): 5 of 8 outliers at 65-95% of runtime; flops have no distinct beat (3 of 3).
- Length: outliers range 14-41s (6 of 8 are 21-41s); flops are 16-21s; length does not separate them.
- Unprompted starts: outliers open already mid-action or already in conversation (7 of 8); flops open with a monologue or crowd with no speech until 9s (flop05) or a self-introduction (flop04, flop06).
- Edit treatment is light: static caption, music, a few cuts (8 of 8 outliers, 3 of 3 flops); the heavy-edit outlier (out12) works on spectacle alone; so editing style is not the differentiator.
- Footage is often a stream or others' camera showing Marlon in a scene rather than Marlon's own selfie (out10, out11, out14, out15; flops 04-06 are selfie or crowd POV): 4 of 8 outliers versus 0 of 3 flops.
