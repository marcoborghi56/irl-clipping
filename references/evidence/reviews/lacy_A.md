## Patterns in this batch
- Outlier moments are an outcome the viewer wants (item won, challenge, phone shatters) or a funny person on camera (shocked face, friend lifted in Shibuya). Flops have no event (flop01) or someone else's event (flop03). 8 of 8 outliers; 0 of 3 flops.
- Hooks state the premise in the first second by caption or spoken line ("Lacy got the Pirate Token", "iPhone Duo durability test", "Me & Marlon are not dating"). Only out04 has no caption, and its action is already in motion. 7 of 8 outliers. flop01 and flop02 captions are weak or mismatched.
- Single-moment clips are short (8-21s). Long out08 (84s) works because it keeps escalating with 62 cuts; flop02 (52s) peaks at 20% then talks for 40s. 7 of 8 outliers under 40s.
- Payoff lands mid-reel (35-84% of runtime) after a visible build. flop02 pays off early (20%); flop01 never does.
- Captions are one static line with an emoji or quotes, never word-by-word subtitles. 7 of 8 outliers.

- Reaction faces (webcam PiP, shocked spectator) carry out01, out02, out05 (3 of 8); real handheld IRL is rare (out03, out04), and its lesson is a spontaneous physical moment, said aloud, in a recognisable place.
- Minimal edit is fine for raw footage: out02, out04, out06 are single takes with no cuts (3 of 8).
