## Patterns in this batch
- Outliers hinge on a legible human beat with a stranger, child or celebrity (compliment to a stranger, a niece's insults, a model's job answer, a puppy and a friend, a crowd mobbing): 8 of 8 outliers; the flops are in-jokes (Silky, Ron, Aura): 3 of 3 flops.
- Hook is a cold open mid-action, usually with the caption present from frame 0: 6 of 8 outliers (out02, out03, out04, out05, out08 had a title or quote caption; out01 and out07 were pure visual arrivals); flops have a caption but no visible tease (3 of 3).
- Payoff arrives early or is visible throughout: 5 of 8 outliers within about 45% of runtime (out02, out04, out05, out08, out03 about 72%); flop payoffs are late or absent (3 of 3).
- Length: 12-25s in 6 of 8 outliers; 55s and 96s exceptions are in-camera continuous footage; flops 17-29s, so length alone does not separate them.
- Edit is light: a single static (often quoted) caption at y 0.75-0.85, square 1080x1080 frame, white-flash cuts, music: 5 of 8 outliers; the flops have the same style, so the treatment does not explain the difference.
- Wordless spectacle (crowds, photographers, paparazzi, a mob) works without sound or caption: 2 of 8 outliers (out01, out07), 0 of 3 flops.
- Laughter spike or an audible reaction exists in the raw audio: 3 of 8 outliers (out06, out08, out04 by the visuals); none flagged in flops.
- Reaction shots of other people (laughing, hands over mouth) cut in right after the payoff: 4 of 8 outliers (out02, out04, out05, out08); 0 of 3 flops.
